NEWSENSE COMPANY — IT & TECHNICAL PROCEDURES GUIDE
Last updated: January 2025

===================================================
1. ONBOARDING — GETTING STARTED WITH YOUR EQUIPMENT
===================================================

Welcome to newSense! On your first day, your equipment will be ready at your desk or shipped to your home address if you are remote. Your IT kit includes a MacBook Pro 14" or equivalent Windows laptop, an external 27" monitor, a wireless keyboard and mouse, and noise-cancelling headphones.

To activate your account:
1. Power on your laptop
2. Connect to the newSense WiFi network (password provided by HR)
3. Open your browser and go to setup.newsense.ai
4. Enter your employee ID (provided in your welcome email)
5. Set up your password following the security policy below

Your corporate email will be firstname.lastname@newsense.ai. You will receive access to Slack, Notion, GitHub, and Google Workspace within 24 hours of account activation.

===================================================
2. PASSWORD & SECURITY POLICY
===================================================

All newSense employees must follow these security rules:

Password requirements:
- Minimum 12 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
- Cannot reuse the last 5 passwords

Passwords must be changed every 90 days. The system will remind you 7 days before expiration.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all accounts. Use Google Authenticator or Authy. Hardware security keys (YubiKey) are available upon request from IT.

Never share your password with anyone, including IT support. newSense IT team will NEVER ask for your password.

===================================================
4. VPN & REMOTE ACCESS
===================================================

When working remotely or on public WiFi, you MUST connect to the newSense VPN before accessing any company resources.

VPN Setup:
1. Download the Cisco AnyConnect client from apps.newsense.ai
2. Enter server address: vpn.newsense.ai
3. Use your corporate email and password
4. Approve the 2FA prompt on your phone

VPN must be active when:
- Accessing internal databases
- Working on client projects
- Using the company GitHub repositories
- Accessing the HR portal

VPN is not required for:
- Using Slack or Google Meet
- Browsing public websites
- Personal use during break time

===================================================
5. DATA & FILE MANAGEMENT
===================================================

All work files must be stored in Google Drive under your department folder. Do not store work files on your local desktop or personal cloud storage (Dropbox, iCloud, etc.).

File naming convention:
[YEAR]-[MONTH]-[PROJECT]-[DESCRIPTION]-[VERSION]
Example: 2025-01-newSense-homepage-redesign-v2.pdf

Sensitive documents (contracts, financial reports, HR files) must be stored in the restricted Google Drive folder and can only be accessed by authorized employees. Contact IT to request access.

Data retention policy:
- Project files: kept for 5 years after project completion
- Client contracts: kept for 10 years
- Financial documents: kept for 7 years
- Employee records: kept for 5 years after leaving the company

===================================================
6. INCIDENT REPORTING
===================================================

If you suspect a security incident (phishing email, lost laptop, unauthorized access), report it immediately:

1. Email: security@newsense.ai (response within 1 hour)
2. Slack: #security-incidents channel
3. Phone: +33 1 23 45 67 90 (24/7 security hotline)

Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 1 hour of discovery. IT will remotely wipe the device immediately to protect company data.

Phishing emails should be forwarded to phishing@newsense.ai and then deleted. Never click links or download attachments from suspicious emails.

===================================================
8. MEETING CULTURE
===================================================

At newSense we follow these meeting rules:

- No meeting Fridays: Fridays are protected focus time. No internal meetings should be scheduled on Fridays unless absolutely necessary.
- Meetings must have an agenda: Send the agenda in the calendar invite. No agenda = decline the meeting.
- Maximum meeting duration: 30 minutes for syncs, 60 minutes for planning sessions, 90 minutes for workshops.
- Start and end on time: Meetings start exactly on time. If you are late by more than 5 minutes, the meeting continues without you.
- Record important meetings: Use Loom to record meetings for team members in different time zones.

All meetings must be scheduled via Google Calendar. Add all necessary participants and include a Google Meet link.

===================================================
10. EQUIPMENT RETURN & OFFBOARDING
===================================================

When leaving newSense, all company equipment must be returned within 5 business days of your last day.

Return process:
1. IT will send you a return shipping label via email
2. Pack all equipment in the original boxes if available
3. Include all accessories (charger, cables, headphones)
4. Drop off at any UPS or Chronopost location

Equipment condition:
- Normal wear and tear is acceptable
- Significant damage will be assessed and deducted from the final paycheck
- Lost equipment will be charged at full replacement cost

Before your last day, IT will:
- Transfer your Google Drive files to your manager
- Archive your emails for 90 days
- Revoke all access credentials immediately on your last day
